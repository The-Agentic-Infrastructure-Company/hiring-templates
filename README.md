# Hiring templates for startups

Free templates for the first hires a startup makes. Each kit has everything needed to run the hire:

- when to hire for the role
- the role in one sentence
- a job description template
- a screening brief (must-haves, either/or profiles, bonuses)
- a four-stage interview plan
- a scorecard
- interview questions
- common mistakes

## Role kits

| Role | The role in one sentence |
|---|---|
| [Founding engineer](roles/founding-engineer.md) | We need a founding engineer: full-stack, TypeScript and Postgres, in New York, who has shipped a product end to end. |
| [First salesperson](roles/first-sales-hire.md) | We need a founding account executive: B2B SaaS, full sales cycle, deals of $10–50k, who has sold at a company before it had a playbook. |
| [First product designer](roles/first-product-designer.md) | We need a founding product designer: has shipped complex web application screens, works directly with engineers, and can do research and visual design alone. |
| [First marketer](roles/first-marketer.md) | We need a first marketer: B2B SaaS, writes well, has grown one channel from nothing to a real share of pipeline, and does the work alone. |
| [First customer success manager](roles/first-customer-success-manager.md) | We need a first customer success manager: B2B SaaS, has onboarded and renewed accounts alone, technical enough to solve problems without an engineer. |
| [First product manager](roles/first-product-manager.md) | We need a first product manager: B2B, has shipped with a small engineering team, talks to customers every week and is comfortable with data and detail. |

## How to use a kit

1. Rewrite the one sentence for your company. If you cannot say the role in one sentence, it is not clear enough to hire for.
2. Fill in the job description and add location, pay range and the interview process.
3. Use the screening brief to decide who gets an interview, yourself or with AI screening. Keep requirements checkable against a resume and about the job only.
4. Run every candidate through the same stages and rate them on the same scorecard before the debrief.
